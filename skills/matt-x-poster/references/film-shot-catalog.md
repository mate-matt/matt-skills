# Film Shot Catalog

This is a curated catalog of 50 top-rated classic movie scene mechanisms for `--style film-dynamic`.

Use these entries as on-screen scene mechanisms only. Do not turn them into film-production sets, director boards, camera crews, rehearsals, clappers, or backstage shoots unless that specific on-screen scene mechanism requires it.

Use film titles only as shorthand for the scene mechanism. Do not recreate exact frames, actor likenesses, protected character names, recognizable costumes, logos, famous dialogue, production credits, ratings, awards, or fictional institutions.

Each film-dynamic runtime module should choose one primary entry and at most one secondary entry.

## 01. The Shawshank Redemption - Torn Poster Wall Reveal

- Scene mechanism: a prison-room authority figure tears back a wall poster and discovers a hidden cavity.
- X insertion: the X post/profile/article card is revealed inside the wall cavity, lit like contraband proof.
- Best for: hidden truth, jailbreak ideas, surprise evidence, long-awaited reveals.
- Avoid: actor likenesses, prison names, exact poster imagery, famous dialogue.
